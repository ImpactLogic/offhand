Offhand 0.12.0

See TESTER-GUIDE.md for setup on Windows 11 with Windower 4,
two Bluetooth Xbox controllers, ViGEmBus and HidHide. Unzip the release
and run Offhand.exe. The self-contained build includes .NET.

To uninstall, exit Offhand from its tray menu before deleting its folder.
To remove your Offhand settings and logs, delete %APPDATA%\ffxi-pad-launcher.
See the tester guide for recovering controller access after a crash.
