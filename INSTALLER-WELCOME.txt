Offhand Setup

Close your game and PlayOnline windows, then choose Exit from Offhand's tray icon before installing or upgrading. Do the same before uninstalling.

Setup installs Offhand, creates desktop and Start menu shortcuts, and adds an entry to Windows Installed apps. Open Offhand from its new shortcut after setup finishes.

Your existing settings and saved sign-in information are kept during upgrades and uninstall. A previous portable copy is left in place; use the new shortcut after installing.

Offhand includes its .NET runtime. Windower, FFXI, ViGEmBus and HidHide are not bundled or installed automatically. Offhand's setup wizard checks your configuration and provides the driver links.
